# Project Rudis — Product Surface

This directory groups public product definitions derived from the Rudis architecture, Canon dependencies and research program.

If you are evaluating Rudis as an investor or strategic partner, begin with the [Investor Read First](../external/Investor_Read_First.md) rather than treating product documents as a complete diligence package.

```text
PRODUCT PACKAGING != CANON CHANGE
PRODUCT PROPOSAL != PRODUCTION READINESS
```

Products may expose, combine or package capabilities already grounded in Rudis, but they do not create constitutional authority by being named, marketed or implemented.

## Product architecture

```text
PROJECT RUDIS
├── StateOS
│   └── RAL / Authority Layer
└── Rudis Habitat
```

- **StateOS** is the broader causal institutional-state and authority architecture.
- **RAL / Rudis Authority Layer** is a specialized authority boundary for consequential execution within the wider StateOS thesis.
- **Rudis Habitat** is the habitable, persistent institutional expression of StateOS across multiple legitimate representations.

## Current product definitions

### 1. [Rudis Habitat](./Rudis_Habitat.md)

The habitable layer of StateOS: a persistent institutional world represented across multiple external Systems, clients and devices while preserving one causal institutional state.

Core thesis:

```text
ONE CAUSAL INSTITUTIONAL STATE
-> MANY LEGITIMATE REPRESENTATIONS
```

Rudis Habitat expands the metaverse concept through institutions, authority, continuity, habitability, education, agentic participation, provenance, justice, economic reciprocity and provider-neutral interoperability.

**Status:** `PRODUCT PROPOSAL / NOT CANON / PRE-D3`.
